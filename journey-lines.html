        How This Works

        Journey Lines is a team-building exercise (credit to Lyssa Adkins) where you turn your life story into one wobbly line and make your teammates feel things. Nobody is grading your drawing skills.

        The line itself is meant to represent your life journey: one continuous path through the highs and lows of your personal and professional life, strung together in whatever order they happened to you.

        1. Draw your line. Click and drag across the canvas like you're sketching an EKG of your life. Ups are good days. Downs are "character development."
        2. Drop some pins. Click an empty spot (or hit "+ Add Milestone") to mark the moments that mattered, like a job, a move, a glorious failure, or a win nobody saw coming. Give it a title, a date, and a photo if you're feeling nostalgic.
        3. Fill in the reflection columns. Learnings: what you'd tell your past self. Triggers: what sets you off, so your team knows when to bring coffee instead of feedback. Leadership Philosophy: the rules you actually live by, not the ones on a poster.
        4. Save to keep working later, or Export Read-Only HTML when you're ready to hand your team a tidy, uneditable copy of your journey.

        One rule: you own the narrative. Add what matters, skip what doesn't, and tell it your own way, just keep it truthful. Nobody else gets a vote on your timeline.
